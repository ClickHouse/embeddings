IVF over the full 99.2M-row table: find the nprobe centroids (of 16,384) nearest the query, then scan only their clusters' RaBitQ codes, keep k × multiplier candidates and rescore them against the full-precision vectors. Centroids are found through their HNSW index, as in the Explorer. That index leaves ~2.5% of them unreachable, which costs ~4 points of recall@100; an exact centroid scan (use_skip_indexes = 0) reaches 0.955 at k = 100, nprobe = 64, ×10 but takes ~160 ms longer per query.
Measured against exact-L2 ground truth over every row, 100 query photos. Green = 1.0, red = 0.0. Second line in each cell is the share of the table read. Not comparable with the codec page, which uses ~100k-row pools: finding the true top-k among 99M rows is harder. The Explorer uses nprobe = 64, k = 100, multiplier ×10.
